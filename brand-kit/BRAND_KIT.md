# Prosodic Brand Kit

## Core Idea

Prosodic is a Linguistics Education App that allows Musicians to use their Lyrics to study Linguistics and Music Craft by turning intuitive talent into metrics they can see, study, and master.

The approved identity pairs the luminous waveform from the final direction board with a sparse measurement halo: art first, analytics second.

The visual source of truth is `brand-kit/approved-final/prosodic-approved-final-direction-board.png`.

Use the background-preserving crops in `brand-kit/approved-final` when presenting the brand direction. Use transparent PNGs only for implementation layers.

## Logo System

- Final symbol: luminous spectrum wave plus outer tick circle.
- Final wave: spectral wave only, no ticks, with a thin baseline measurement line.
- Support element: faded dot grid or faded line grid.
- Premium treatment: liquid-light glow, not chunky chrome.
- Production fallback: flat white or flat gradient 2D mark.

### Gradient Blend

Primary wave gradient:

- `#FF6B5E` at 0%
- `#E045A8` at 42%
- `#7B61FF` at 70%
- `#5BA7FF` at 100%
- Direction: left to right, 0 degrees.

Halo gradient:

- `#FF6B5E` lower left
- `#E045A8` upper left

- `#5BA7FF` faded right edge
- Direction: circular sweep, right side fades to 30 to 40 percent opacity.

## Typography

All fonts are Google Fonts and free for commercial use.

- Display and wordmark direction: `Space Grotesk`
- Interface and body: `Inter`
- Optional numeric metrics: `Sora`

## Tone

- Premium but useful.
- Artistic before technical.
- Clear, direct, and encouraging.
- Never academic, never gimmicky.

## Light Mode

- Background: `#F5F2EA`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Card: `#EEF1F7`
- Primary text: `#08090E`
- Muted text: `rgba(8, 9, 14, 0.58)`
- Hairline: `rgba(8, 9, 14, 0.10)`
- Accent: use the same gradient, slightly less glow.

## Usage Rules

- Use the full wave plus tick halo for splash, monthly updates, app icon, and hero moments.
- Use wave-only for small UI marks, headers, and loading states.
- Use flat white when the logo must be clean, tiny, or accessible.
- Use the faded grid only as background texture, never as the main logo.
- Do not use microphones, headphones, music notes, pen nibs, heartbeat lines, or generic AI sparkle marks.
- Do not return to the earlier P-shaped logo direction. The approved direction is the spectrum wave inside the metric halo.
