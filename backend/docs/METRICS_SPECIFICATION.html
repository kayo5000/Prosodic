1. Architectural Framework & Normalization

All raw engine outputs are mapped onto a unified, comparable scale through domain/final_result_converter.py, ensuring multi-engine blends do not distort or invert relative weights.

  • Standard Scale Output Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Continuous).
  • Standard Polarity: Always normal (higher numerical value = stronger craft expression).
  • Clipping: Clamped defensively to [0.0, 1.0].

2. Rhyme & Phonetic Architecture

Core Thresholds & Classifications

  • Phoneme Rhyme Score: Continuous float in [0.0, 1.0] measuring phonetic alignment.
  • Rhyme Edge Threshold (RHYME_THRESHOLD = 0.78): Minimum similarity score required to establish a valid rhyme edge in graph clustering.
  • Near-Rhyme Same Vowel Score (NEAR_RHYME_SAME_VOWEL_SCORE = 0.75): Baseline for near-rhymes sharing vowel base with different stress digit or rhotic coloring (e.g. ER1 vs ER2).

Qualitative Rhyme Classifications (RhymeType)

  • PERFECT — Identical vowel nucleus and identical coda consonants (e.g., mind / blind).
  • SLANT — Shared vowel nucleus with phonetically proximate coda consonants.
  • NEAR — Shared vowel with minor coda divergence.
  • FAMILY — Membership in the same perceptual vowel sound family.
  • NONE — Disparate phonetics (<0.78).

AAVE Phonological Transformations (aave_phonology.py)

  1. Consonant Cluster Reduction: Word-final stop deleted in homorganic clusters (e.g. /P AE1 S T/ → /P AE1 S/ allowing past to rhyme with class; hand → man).
  2. Monophthongization: Glide weakening of /AY/ → /AA/ (e.g. /T AY1 M/ → /T AA1 M/ allowing time to rhyme with calm; mine → man).
  3. Postvocalic R-Vocalization: Deletion of rhotic codas (e.g. /M AO1 R/ → /M AO1/ rhyming with saw; /ER/ → /AH/ merging bird and bud).

3. Syllable & Rhyme Density

Density Component Formulas

Internal Rhyme Density (%) = (Highlighted Rhyme Syllables / Total Syllables) × 100
Multisyllabic Density (%) = (Deduplicated Compound Syllables / Total Syllables) × 100
Motif Recurrence Density (%) = (Distinct Colors in Bar / Total Palette in Verse) × 100

Syllabic Load & Pocketing Control

  • Syllables Per Beat (SPB): Syllable count distributed across quarter-note pulses.
  • Density Control Window: Optimal hip-hop pocket sits in 2.5 – 4.5 SPB.

Density Gradient Arc Shapes (ArcType)

  • BUILDING — Syllabic load rises steadily toward the close of a section.
  • RELEASING — Syllabic load descends from high density to provide breath and resolution.
  • FLAT — Variance across bars is <20% (FLAT_VARIANCE_RATIO = 0.20).
  • PEAK_AND_RELEASE — Sustained high-density peak (≥25% duration) followed by open release.
  • COMPLEX — Undulating or multi-peak density profile.

4. Cadence, Pocket & Beat Grid Mechanics

The 16-Step Beat Grid

  • Resolution (GRID_SIZE = 16): 16 sixteenth-note slots per 4/4 bar.
  • Strong Downbeats: Positions 0, 4, 8, 12 (Beats 1, 2, 3, 4).
  • The Pocket: Positions 4 and 12 (Snare downbeats on Beats 2 and 4).
  • Tolerances: POCKET_WINDOW = 1 (±1 sixteenth note); NUDGE_WINDOW = 2.

Flow Signatures

  • On-Grid — >75% of stressed syllables land on strong downbeats (0, 4, 8, 12).
  • Syncopated — >50% of stressed syllables land on the snare pocket (4, 12).
  • Floating — <35% on-beat alignment; decoupled from the rigid pulse.
  • Pocket Jumper — Dynamic alternation between on-beat landing and syncopated runs.

Cadence Signal Taxonomy (Stress Mismatches)

Signal Identifier Trigger Condition Craft Meaning
promotion Lexically weak syllable (0) lands on stressed grid position (≥1). Performed rhythmic emphasis on structural word.
demotion Lexically stressed syllable (1, 2) lands off-beat within POCKET_WINDOW. Softened delivery adjacent to the downbeat.
syncopation Lexically stressed syllable lands off-beat beyond POCKET_WINDOW. Deliberate rhythmic displacement between pulses.
trochaic_inversion Rising grid prominence over falling lexical stress. Metrical reversal creating expressive tension.
stress_clash Adjacent lexically stressed syllables colliding without unstressed buffer. Trigger environment for English's Rhythm Rule.
stress_lapse ≥3 consecutive unstressed syllables (LAPSE_RUN_LENGTH = 3). Rhythmic lull or rapid syllabic patter.
secondary_recruitment Secondary stress (2) lands on strong beat while primary (1) does not. Anchoring rhythm on secondary word syllable.
level_stress_ambiguity Word has multiple dictionary pronunciations with contested stress. Lexical ambiguity in CMU dictionary.

Deliberateness Gate

  • likely_automatic — Occurs within a stress_clash context (automatic Rhythm Rule retraction).
  • possible_deliberate — Word recurs on ≥2 other lines without clash context (RECURRENCE_MIN = 2).
  • uncertain — Standard baseline; under-claims intent to avoid false assertions.

Boundary Detection Rules (phrase_container_engine.py)

A structural container break is declared when cumulative signal weight exceeds BOUNDARY_THRESHOLD = 1.5 and at least MIN_SIGNALS_FIRED = 2 individual signals fire:

rhyme_resolution 0.8 Terminal rhyme scheme completes and resolves on this bar.

line_length_shift 0.4 Line word count differs by >4 words from previous (LINE_LENGTH_SHIFT_WORD_DIFF = 4).

6. Visual Motifs & Pattern Reading

Perceptual Family Activity Score Formula

Activity Score = (Member Count Norm × 0.40) + (Line Spread × 0.35) + (Stress Ratio × 0.25)

Where:
• Member Count Norm = min(Members, 10) / 10
• Line Spread = Unique Lines with Family / Total Lines
• Stress Ratio = Stressed Members / Total Members
  • Active Family Threshold: Activity Score ≥ 0.25 confirms intentional deployment.
  • Parallel Pocketing: Flagged when top 2 active families have activity scores within 15% of each other.
  • Motif Cluster Strength (cluster_strength): Continuous float in [0.0, 1.0] measuring semantic tightness around an anchor word.

7. Semantics, Concreteness & Suggestions

Lexical & Semantic Dimensions

  • Semantic Similarity (semantic_similarity): Cosine distance in [0.0, 1.0] using spaCy en_core_web_md vectors.
  • Brysbaert Concreteness Score: Scale of 1.0 – 5.0 (1.0 = abstract concepts like "freedom"; 5.0 = tangible objects like "brick").

Suggestion Composite Ranking Score (suggestion_engine.py)

Composite Score = (Rhyme Score × 0.55) + (Thesaurus Score × 0.30) + (Syllable Priority × 0.15)
  • Rhyme Score (55%): Raw phonetic similarity scaled to 0–100.
  • Thesaurus Score (30%): Moby Thesaurus depth tiers: 0, 30–70, 85, 90, 100.
  • Syllable Priority (15%): 0 = off-target, 1 = ±1 syllable, 2 = exact syllable match.

9. Phonoaffective Signature Framework

Formulated in aspiration_gap.py to measure emotional encoding and acoustic intentionality.

The 8-Component Formula

E = (PT × w1) + (PP × w2) + (SF × w3) + (CM × w4) + (IS × w5) + (TP × w6) + (SA × w7) + (SD × w8)
  • PT (Phonemic Texture): Ratio of plosives/hard consonants vs liquids/fricatives, vowel openness.
  • PP (Prosodic Pressure): Syllable density, delivery compression, stress inversion rate.
  • SF (Semantic Field Gravity): Semantic valence, imagery clustering, thematic cohesion.
  • CM (Cultural Memory): Vernacular frequency, regional marker density, AAVE bridges.
  • IS (Intentionality Signal): Revision count, dictionary override telemetry.
  • TP (Temporal Position): Section trajectory (Verse 1 setup vs Climax vs Outro).
  • SA (Sonic Aspiration): Selected emotional target from the 15 canonical labels (aggressive, celebratory, contemplative, defiant, desperate, grieving, hopeful, hungry, melancholic, nostalgic, peaceful, proud, raw, triumphant, vulnerable).
  • SD (Subtext Delta): Discrepancy between surface semantic sentiment and acoustic tension.
  • Aspiration Gap Score (aspiration_gap_score): Euclidean distance bounded in [0.0, 1.0] (0.0 = perfect alignment).

10. Longitudinal Mastery & Progress Tracking

Evaluated across catalog history in mastery_engine.py.

Activation Gate (Unlock Thresholds)

  • ≥3 distinct songs analyzed
  • ≥40 total rhyme events recorded
  • ≥20 total cadence events recorded
  • ≥15 total bars analyzed

Proficiency Tiers

  • Developing — 0 to 39
  • Approaching — 40 to 64
  • Competent — 65 to 81
  • Exemplary — 82 to 100
